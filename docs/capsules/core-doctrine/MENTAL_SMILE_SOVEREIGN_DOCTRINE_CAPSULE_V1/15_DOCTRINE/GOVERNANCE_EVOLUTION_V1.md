Status: CERTIFIED

## Purpose

Explain governance evolution across Generation 1.

## Governance Evolution

Mental Smile began with product ambition and grew into constitutional governance. The reason was not bureaucracy. The reason was survival.

As the platform accumulated rooms, sections, routes, signals, and docs, the risk changed from "can we build?" to "can we know what is true?"

Governance evolved in layers:

1. Section boundaries.
2. Owner authority.
3. Registries.
4. Cards.
5. Digital twins.
6. Code Authority.
7. Code Gate.
8. Active Documents.
9. Capsules.

## Legal Evolution

Legal doctrine moved from broad caution to explicit responsibility:

- no credentials in capsules
- no private keys
- no user data
- no diagnosis claims
- no medical claims in support links
- no direct sensitive runtime write without governance
- no historical evidence promoted as current policy

## Ethical Evolution

Ethics moved from good intention to enforceable structure:

- anonymous signals
- privacy classes
- retention classes
- owner review
- quarantine for broken language
- no identity leakage
- no diagnosis inference

## Technical Evolution

Technical architecture moved from screens/routes to coded runtime pathways:

- section code
- plug code
- archive destination
- magnet
- package family
- Firestore rules
- intake/guard/resolution doctrine

## Economic Evolution

Economic thinking moved from "build more features" to "build reusable constitutional assets."

Capsules reduce future rebuild cost, onboarding cost, AI correction cost, and accidental rework cost.

The economic value of doctrine is lower entropy.
