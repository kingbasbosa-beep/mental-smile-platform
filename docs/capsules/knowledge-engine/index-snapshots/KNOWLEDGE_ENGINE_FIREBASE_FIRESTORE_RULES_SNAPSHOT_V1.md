## Architecture Rules

- Question-first: knowledge starts from a well-formed question, not a fixed answer.
- Dialogue-first: knowledge can expose uncertainty, context, competing explanations, and next questions.
- No medical bias: do not privilege a claim because it sounds clinical, popular, commercial, or institutional.
- No single school bias: multiple schools and scientific viewpoints may coexist when clearly classified.
- No commercial bias: products, apps, organizations, and resources are discovery objects, not endorsements.
- Evidence hierarchy is visible and challengeable.
- Consensus and disagreement are both first-class knowledge states.
- Sources require citation, owner, review status, jurisdiction/scope, and retirement/update lifecycle.
- Knowledge content is not stored in this capsule; only the governing architecture is stored.

## Future Capsule Must Exclude

- Full books, articles, clinical protocols, or copyrighted source content.
- Diagnosis, treatment recommendation, personal data, or user-specific health inference.
- Legacy or placeholder documents as active truth.
- Runtime data, Firestore documents, credentials, secrets, and private keys.
