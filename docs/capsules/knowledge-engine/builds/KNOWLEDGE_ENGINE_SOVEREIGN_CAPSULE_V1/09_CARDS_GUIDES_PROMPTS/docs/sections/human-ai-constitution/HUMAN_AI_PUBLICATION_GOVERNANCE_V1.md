# HUMAN_AI_PUBLICATION_GOVERNANCE_V1

Status: CURRENT

Owning Layer: Human-AI Constitution

Purpose: Define publication governance for official accounts, website, store listings, social media, email campaigns, press releases, and partner communications.

## Publication Surfaces

Governed publication applies to:

- Mental Smile official accounts
- Website
- Store listings
- Social media
- Email campaigns
- Press releases
- Partner communications
- Provider public profiles
- Organization public profiles
- Public library content
- Media kits
- Official letters

## Required Publication Chain

Every publication must pass:

1. Approval
2. Brand validation
3. Safety validation
4. Medical validation when relevant
5. Archive record
6. Audit record
7. Publication registry entry

## Official Accounts

No user may publish directly to Mental Smile official accounts.

AI may draft, adapt, translate, format, or suggest.

Publication requires human review and approval.

## Website

Website content must be accurate, brand-aligned, safe, accessible, and archived.

Provider or organization materials must be clearly scoped and must not imply endorsement beyond approved wording.

## Store Listings

Store listing text must avoid unsupported claims, medical promises, misleading outcomes, and hidden capabilities.

## Social Media

Social content must pass:

- Mission fit
- Brand voice
- Public decency
- Medical claim check
- Copyright check
- Conflict check
- Approval gate

## Email Campaigns

Email campaigns must identify purpose, audience, source, sender, approval owner, and archive destination.

No campaign may be sent from AI output alone.

## Press Releases

Press releases require Owner approval and evidence review.

Claims about partnerships, metrics, impact, product readiness, or official relationships must be verified.

## Partner Communications

Partner communications must preserve official tone, authority boundaries, and evidence.

No AI-generated partner claim becomes official without approval.

## Publication Registry Fields

Every approved public item should record:

- Title
- Surface
- Owner
- Source draft
- AI involvement
- Reviewers
- Approval status
- Medical validation status
- Brand validation status
- Archive location
- Publication date
- Retention status

No AI Connections: YES

No Execution: YES
