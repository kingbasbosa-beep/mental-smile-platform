# HUMAN_AI_AUTHORITY_RULES_V1

Status: CURRENT

Owning Layer: Human-AI Constitution

Purpose: Define authority permissions and boundaries for humans, AI systems, organizational assets, official resources, and review gates.

## Authority Registry

| Authority | May Do | Must Not Do | Requires Review When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human Authority | Request support, provide intent, review outputs | Bypass rules, misuse platform, impersonate roles | Public impact or safety risk exists |
| AI Authority | Draft, classify, summarize, recommend | Approve, publish, diagnose, restrict, punish | Any output leaves private draft status |
| Owner Authority | Final decision, override, approve constitutional direction | Use approval to bypass safety or legal obligations | High risk or external exposure exists |
| Provider Authority | Submit raw professional data, request profile/content support | Fake credentials, diagnosis claims, direct official account posting | Public profile/content is generated |
| Organization Authority | Submit official materials, request documents, prepare communications | Make unsupported claims, misuse Mental Smile channels | External communication is planned |
| Client Authority | Reflection, support language, navigation, accessibility, discovery | Self-diagnosis, medication advice, crisis replacement | Harm, crisis, or diagnosis request appears |
| Approval Authority | Validate progression from draft to active | Rubber-stamp unsafe outputs | Publication, official communication, medical claim |
| Publication Authority | Release approved public material | Publish unapproved or unsupported material | Always |
| Memory Authority | Retain approved governed memory | Retain sensitive or unsupported data as truth | Memory affects future outputs |
| Knowledge Authority | Use appropriate source material | Provide unbounded or role-inappropriate knowledge | Scientific, legal, medical, or policy content appears |
| Prompt Authority | Structure AI instructions | Override constitution or safety | Tool scope changes |
| Reasoning Authority | Explain, evaluate, recommend | Decide without review | Risk, policy, or public action exists |
| Conversation Authority | Maintain safe dialogue and role boundaries | Encourage unsafe reliance or harmful loops | Crisis, abuse, or diagnosis indicators appear |
| Audit Authority | Record public-impact AI actions | Hide material actions | Any governed action occurs |
| Privacy Authority | Protect sensitive content | Expose private content | Personal, provider, or evidence data appears |
| Safety Authority | Stop or redirect unsafe flows | Trade safety for speed | Harm, child safety, abuse, or emergency indicators appear |
| Compliance Authority | Enforce law/policy/copyright/medical claim limits | Ignore regulatory implications | Official or public material is involved |
| Escalation Authority | Route high-risk matters to responsible human layer | Escalate casually or expose data unnecessarily | Risk exceeds normal workflow |
| Decision Authority | Determine proceed, pause, reject, review, archive | Let AI self-approve | Any governed decision exists |

## Core Rule

Authority must be explicit.

If authority is unclear, the output remains draft-only and must not be published, enforced, remembered, or treated as approved.
