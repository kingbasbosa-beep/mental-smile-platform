# HUMAN_AI_TOOL_BOUNDARIES_V1

Status: CURRENT

Owning Layer: Human-AI Constitution

Purpose: Define allowed domains, blocked domains, role-based tool access, capability limits, audit trail, abuse prevention, misuse detection, account misuse prevention, and brand protection.

## Allowed Domains

Mental Smile tools may support:

- Mental health education
- Emotional support language
- Accessibility
- Navigation
- Provider profile preparation
- Organization communication
- Public awareness
- Governance support
- Operational drafting
- Documentation
- Archive preparation
- Brand-safe publication preparation

## Blocked Domains

Tools must not support:

- Diagnosis
- Medication advice
- Treatment planning
- Crisis replacement
- Sexual misuse
- Offensive content
- Hate speech
- Political campaigning
- Religious conflict
- Credential fabrication
- Account misuse
- Unauthorized publishing
- Unrelated commercial promotion
- Provider comparison claims
- Unsupported medical claims

## Role Based Tool Access

Client:

- Support, discovery, reflection, accessibility, navigation.

Provider:

- Profile writing, educational content, professional assets, awareness material.

Organization:

- Partnership documents, operational reports, official communication drafts.

Owner:

- Full supervised scope.

AI Member:

- Internal draft, classification, review, and recommendation scope only.

## Capability Limits

Every tool must declare:

- Intended users
- Allowed outputs
- Blocked outputs
- Required approval level
- Audit requirement
- Publication status
- Retention status

## Audit Trail

Audit trail is required when outputs affect:

- Public content
- Provider profile
- Organization profile
- Official communication
- Platform accounts
- Governance decision
- Evidence
- Safety escalation

## Abuse Prevention

The system must detect and block:

- Sexual misuse
- Offensive language
- Irrelevant commercial use
- Unauthorized publishing
- Brand misuse
- Unsafe medical claims
- Self-diagnosis loops
- Provider misuse
- Account misuse
- Non-domain requests

## Misuse Detection

Potential misuse indicators:

- User requests direct official posting
- User asks AI to invent credentials
- User asks for diagnosis certainty
- User requests harmful instructions
- User asks to target or harass a person or group
- User tries to bypass approval
- User asks for non-mission commercial advertising

## Account Misuse Prevention

Official accounts are protected resources.

No credentials are exposed to users or AI.

AI may prepare content but may not publish, schedule, or authenticate official accounts.

## Brand Protection

AI must protect Mental Smile from:

- False promises
- Medical overclaiming
- Inconsistent tone
- Unsafe positioning
- Unauthorized endorsements
- Misleading partnerships
- Low-quality public materials

No AI Connections: YES

No Execution: YES
