# HUMAN_AI_USAGE_LOGIC_V1

Status: CURRENT

Owning Layer: Human-AI Constitution

Purpose: Define complete interaction logic between clients, providers, organizations, owner, AI systems, and human-to-human communication mediated by AI.

## Client To AI

Allowed:

- Emotional support language
- Reflection prompts
- Understanding feelings
- Platform navigation
- Resource discovery
- Accessibility assistance
- Motivational writing
- Simplified explanation of public resources

Blocked:

- Self-diagnosis
- Medication advice
- Treatment planning
- Crisis replacement
- Provider ranking
- Deep scientific material intended for specialists
- Replacing professional care

Required response when blocked:

- Validate the user without confirming diagnosis
- Redirect toward education and professional guidance
- Escalate safety concerns when needed

## Provider To AI

Allowed:

- Professional profile writing
- Public awareness content
- Educational material
- Profile improvement
- Bio and CV preparation
- Accessibility formatting
- Research summary within mental health and support fields

Blocked:

- Personal unrelated business
- Non-mental-health advertising
- Offensive, sexual, hateful, political, or religious conflict content
- Client-specific diagnosis generation
- Fake credentials
- Unsupported medical claims
- Direct publication on official accounts

## Organization To AI

Allowed:

- Partnership drafts
- Organizational profile material
- Official letters
- Meeting minutes
- Executive summaries
- Public education content
- Operational reports

Blocked:

- Unsupported institutional claims
- Misuse of Mental Smile brand
- Unapproved partner announcements
- Unverified service promises
- Public policy claims without review

## Owner To AI

Allowed:

- Full supervised strategic, operational, constitutional, publication, and review support

Still required:

- Safety validation
- Legal/compliance awareness
- Archive record for public-impact decisions
- Manual approval for publication

## AI To AI

Allowed in future governed phases:

- Draft comparison
- Prompt review
- Knowledge matching
- Risk flagging
- Output critique
- Alternative generation

Blocked:

- Self-approval
- Autonomous publication
- Autonomous account access
- Independent escalation outside governed workflow
- Background execution without explicit runtime authority

## Human To Human Through AI

Allowed:

- Tone improvement
- Accessibility rewriting
- Translation
- Summarization
- Drafting respectful replies
- Structuring official messages

Blocked:

- Manipulation
- Impersonation
- Hidden authorship in official claims
- Unsafe advice
- Publishing on behalf of another person without permission

## Universal Interaction Fields

Every governed AI interaction should be classifiable by:

- User role
- Intended output
- Tool or capability requested
- Allowed domain
- Blocked domain
- Publication target
- Approval requirement
- Audit status
- Safety status

No AI Connections: YES

No Execution: YES
