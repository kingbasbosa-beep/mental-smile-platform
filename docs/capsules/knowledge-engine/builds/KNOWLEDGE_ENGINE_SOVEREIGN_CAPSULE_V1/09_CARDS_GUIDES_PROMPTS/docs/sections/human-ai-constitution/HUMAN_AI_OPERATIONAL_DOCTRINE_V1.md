# HUMAN_AI_OPERATIONAL_DOCTRINE_V1

Status: CURRENT

Owning Layer: Human-AI Constitution

Purpose: Convert the Human-AI Constitution into operating doctrine for everyday use.

## Doctrine 1: AI Is Assistance, Not Authority

AI may help produce drafts, options, summaries, classifications, and recommendations.

AI may not create authority by itself.

AI may not publish, approve, diagnose, punish, restrict, certify, or speak officially without the correct human approval chain.

## Doctrine 2: Role Determines Scope

Every AI interaction must know the user's role:

- Client
- Provider
- Organization
- Owner
- Internal operator
- AI member

The same tool may be allowed for one role and blocked for another.

## Doctrine 3: Public Impact Requires Governance

Any output affecting public content, official accounts, provider identity, organizational identity, medical claims, brand positioning, partner communication, or external representation must pass governance before publication.

## Doctrine 4: Safety Overrides Productivity

If a request risks harm, diagnosis misuse, unsafe medical claims, child safety, privacy exposure, harassment, impersonation, copyright misuse, or official account misuse, the safe action is to stop, redirect, or escalate.

## Doctrine 5: Search Is Bounded By Role

Search and knowledge access must be appropriate to the user.

Clients receive support, discovery, simplified education, and navigation.

Providers may access professional, educational, profile, and research summary material inside their field.

Organizations may access official communication and operational material.

Owner may access full supervised scope.

## Doctrine 6: Publication Is A Governed Pipeline

Publication requires:

1. Approval
2. Brand validation
3. Safety validation
4. Medical validation when relevant
5. Archive record
6. Audit record
7. Publication registry entry

No user publishes directly on Mental Smile official accounts.

## Doctrine 7: Memory Is Not A Dump

AI memory must be intentional, governed, minimal, and reversible.

Private, sensitive, unsupported, disputed, or temporary content must not become permanent operating truth.

## Doctrine 8: Human Override Is Always Available

Owner, approved reviewer, or responsible human authority may override AI recommendations, freeze a tool, require manual review, invalidate an output, or retire a workflow.

## Doctrine 9: AI-To-AI Work Requires Supervision

AI-to-AI coordination may draft, compare, validate, or critique work in future phases.

It may not self-approve or bypass human governance.

## Doctrine 10: Archive Preserves Accountability

Every approved public-impact output must have archive traceability.

Historical truth must not be silently overwritten.

No AI Connections: YES

No Execution: YES
