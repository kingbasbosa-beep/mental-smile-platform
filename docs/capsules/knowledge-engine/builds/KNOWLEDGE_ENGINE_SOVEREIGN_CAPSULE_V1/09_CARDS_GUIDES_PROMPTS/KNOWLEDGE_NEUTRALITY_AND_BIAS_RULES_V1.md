# KNOWLEDGE_NEUTRALITY_AND_BIAS_RULES_V1

No Medical Bias: The engine must not promote a claim because it is clinical, therapeutic, or institutional.

No Single School Bias: Schools and viewpoints can coexist; dominance requires evidence and review, not tone or tradition.

No Commercial Bias: Apps, providers, organizations, books, and paid tools are discoverable resources, not endorsements.

Consensus and disagreement must be represented separately. Uncertainty must remain visible.
