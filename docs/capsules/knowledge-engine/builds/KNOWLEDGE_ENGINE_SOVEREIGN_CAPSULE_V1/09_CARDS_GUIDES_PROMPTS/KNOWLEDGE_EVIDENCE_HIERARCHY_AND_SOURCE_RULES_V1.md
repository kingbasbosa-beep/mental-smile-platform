# KNOWLEDGE_EVIDENCE_HIERARCHY_AND_SOURCE_RULES_V1

## Evidence Classes

- Meta-analysis
- Systematic review
- Clinical/scientific guideline
- Peer-reviewed research
- Books and academic texts
- Articles and expert commentary
- Organization resource
- Government resource
- Community resource
- External app/resource listing
- Recovery/accessibility support resource

## Rules

Evidence class does not equal truth. Every source requires citation, publication/update date if available, organization/author, jurisdiction/scope, viewpoint, review status, conflict/commercial status, and next review date.

No source is recommended treatment by default. Resources are discovery-only unless a future Owner-approved policy says otherwise.
