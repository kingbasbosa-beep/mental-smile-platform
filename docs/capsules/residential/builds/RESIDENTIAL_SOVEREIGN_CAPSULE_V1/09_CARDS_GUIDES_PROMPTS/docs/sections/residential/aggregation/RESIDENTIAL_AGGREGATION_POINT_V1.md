Residential Aggregation Point converts Residential signals, Residential reports, and Residential activity summaries into reviewed Residential Summary Packages for Strategic Planning and Intelligence.

- Residential Reports.

Residential Aggregation Point is the first Residential-owned intelligence source eligible to feed Strategic Planning and Intelligence.

It provides reviewed summaries only.
