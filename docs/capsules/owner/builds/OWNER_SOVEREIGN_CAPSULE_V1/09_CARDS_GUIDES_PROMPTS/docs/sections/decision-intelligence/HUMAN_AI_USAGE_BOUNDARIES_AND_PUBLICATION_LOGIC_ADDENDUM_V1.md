# HUMAN_AI_USAGE_BOUNDARIES_AND_PUBLICATION_LOGIC_ADDENDUM_V1

Status: CURRENT

Owning Layer: Decision Intelligence

Purpose: Define how clients, service providers, organizations, and internal users may use AI-powered tools inside Mental Smile.

This addendum prevents scope confusion, misuse of platform tools, unsafe content generation, brand misuse, unauthorized publication, and non-relevant activity on Mental Smile accounts.

## 1. Client AI Usage Logic

Clients may use AI tools only for:
- Emotional support language
- Reflection prompts
- Understanding feelings
- Platform navigation
- Resource discovery
- Accessibility assistance
- Motivational writing
- Simplified explanation of public resources

Clients must not use AI tools for:
- Self-diagnosis
- Medication advice
- Treatment planning
- Crisis replacement
- Provider ranking
- Deep scientific material intended for specialists
- Replacing professional care

If a client asks for diagnosis, AI must redirect toward support, education, and professional guidance.

## 2. Provider AI Usage Logic

Service providers may use AI tools only for:
- Professional profile writing
- Public awareness content
- Educational material
- Profile improvement
- Bio and CV preparation
- Public communication
- Arabic simplification
- Accessibility formatting
- Research summary within mental health and support fields

Providers must not use platform AI tools for:
- Personal unrelated business
- Non-mental-health commercial advertising
- Offensive content
- Sexual content
- Political campaigning
- Religious conflict content
- Client-specific diagnosis generation
- Fake credentials
- Medical claims without source
- Publishing directly to Mental Smile public accounts without approval

## 3. Platform Account Publishing Logic

No user may publish directly on Mental Smile official accounts without approval.

Every publishable item must pass:
- Scope Check
- Brand Voice Check
- Safety Check
- Medical Claim Check
- Source Check
- Public Decency Check
- Conflict Check
- Approval Gate
- Archive Record

Blocked publication categories:
- Personal ads
- Unrelated products
- Sexual content
- Insults
- Hate speech
- Political campaigning
- Religious conflict
- Diagnosis claims
- Treatment promises
- Provider comparison claims
- Unverified medical claims
- Content outside Mental Smile mission

## 4. Tool Scope Logic

Every AI tool request must include:
- User role
- Requested tool
- Intended output
- Allowed domain
- Blocked domain
- Publication target
- Approval requirement
- Audit status

Role boundaries:
- Client: Support, discovery, reflection, accessibility.
- Provider: Professional writing, educational content, profile assets, awareness.
- Organization: Partnership, reporting, documents, official communication.
- Owner: Full supervised scope.

## 5. Abuse Prevention Logic

The system must detect and block:
- Sexual misuse
- Offensive language
- Irrelevant commercial use
- Unauthorized publishing
- Brand misuse
- Unsafe medical claims
- Self-diagnosis loops
- Provider misuse
- Account misuse
- Non-domain requests

## 6. Audit Requirement

Every AI-assisted action that may affect public content, provider profile, official communication, or platform accounts must be auditable.

Audit fields:
- User
- Role
- Tool
- Request category
- Output category
- Approval status
- Publication status
- Timestamp
- Reviewer
- Final decision

## Final Rule

Mental Smile AI tools are not open general-purpose toys.

They are governed support, communication, education, accessibility, and operational tools.

Every use must remain inside Mental Smile mission, safety boundaries, and constitutional logic.
