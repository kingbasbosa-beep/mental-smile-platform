# HUMAN_AI_CONSTITUTION_V1

Status: CURRENT

Owning Layer: Human-AI Constitution

Generation: Generation Two

Block: G2.5-B

Purpose: Define the constitutional relationship between humans, AI systems, organizational assets, official Mental Smile resources, and publication authority.

## Constitutional Principle

Mental Smile treats AI as governed operational assistance.

AI does not own authority.

AI does not replace humans.

AI does not publish on behalf of Mental Smile without approval.

AI does not create clinical, legal, financial, organizational, or public authority by itself.

Human authority remains sovereign.

Owner authority remains final.

Every AI-assisted action must remain inside Mental Smile mission, safety boundaries, privacy expectations, and audit logic.

## Authority Layers

### Human Authority

Humans remain the source of consent, intention, accountability, and final responsibility.

Humans may request support, drafting, discovery, explanation, reflection, and operational assistance.

Humans may not use AI to bypass governance, impersonate authority, fabricate credentials, publish unsafe claims, or misuse Mental Smile assets.

### AI Authority

AI may assist, organize, summarize, draft, classify, recommend, and explain.

AI may not approve, publish, diagnose, punish, restrict, escalate externally, contact official entities, or claim institutional authority.

AI authority is always delegated, bounded, logged, reversible, and reviewable.

### Owner Authority

Owner authority is sovereign over project direction, final publication permission, constitutional amendments, official account use, public positioning, and platform-level decisions.

Owner authority may override AI outputs, reject recommendations, freeze tools, require review, or retire a process.

### Provider Authority

Providers own their raw professional information and may request AI support for profile writing, educational communication, accessibility formatting, and public awareness content.

Providers may not use AI to create false credentials, diagnosis claims, treatment promises, comparison claims, or unauthorized publication on Mental Smile official accounts.

### Organization Authority

Organizations may use AI-assisted tools for official communication, profile materials, partnership documents, educational content, and operational reports.

Organizations may not publish institutional claims through Mental Smile without validation, evidence, and approval.

### Client Authority

Clients may use AI support for reflection, emotional language, navigation, accessibility assistance, resource discovery, and simplified understanding.

Clients may not use Mental Smile AI for self-diagnosis, medication guidance, treatment planning, crisis replacement, provider ranking, or replacing professional care.

### Approval Authority

Approval authority governs whether an output may move from draft to active use.

Approval may include safety, brand, medical, legal, compliance, owner, technical, or archive validation depending on scope.

### Publication Authority

Publication authority governs public accounts, website content, store listings, social channels, email campaigns, press releases, partner communications, and public documents.

No publication is valid without approval, brand validation, safety validation, medical validation when relevant, archive record, audit trail, and publication registry entry.

### Memory Authority

Memory authority governs what may be retained, reused, referenced, or forgotten.

AI memory must not preserve private, sensitive, unsupported, or unauthorized user content as operational truth.

### Knowledge Authority

Knowledge authority governs what source material may guide AI-assisted outputs.

Scientific, medical, legal, policy, and organizational knowledge must remain source-aware, bounded, and appropriate to role.

### Prompt Authority

Prompt authority governs instructions given to AI.

Prompts must not override constitutional restrictions, safety rules, role boundaries, or publication requirements.

### Reasoning Authority

Reasoning authority permits AI to explain, compare, flag risk, and recommend.

Reasoning does not become decision authority unless reviewed and approved by the correct human role.

### Conversation Authority

Conversation authority governs tone, safety, role boundaries, escalation triggers, and user protection during AI interactions.

AI must redirect unsafe requests and clarify limits when needed.

### Audit Authority

Audit authority requires records for AI-assisted actions that affect public content, provider profiles, official communication, governance, publication, or user safety.

### Privacy Authority

Privacy authority protects private user data, provider data, organizational data, communication records, evidence, identity materials, and sensitive context.

### Safety Authority

Safety authority overrides convenience, speed, publication pressure, and user preference when risk appears.

### Compliance Authority

Compliance authority governs laws, platform policies, copyright, medical claims, child safety, accessibility obligations, official communication, and partner commitments.

### Escalation Authority

Escalation authority routes high-risk matters to Owner, Legal, Technical, Governance Operations, Archive, Monitoring, or emergency channels where appropriate.

### Decision Authority

Decision authority determines whether a proposed action may proceed, pause, require review, be rejected, or be archived.

AI may support decision preparation, but does not become the final decision-maker.

## Final Constitutional Rule

Mental Smile AI is a governed operating capability.

It supports humans.

It protects the mission.

It never becomes an unbounded authority layer.
