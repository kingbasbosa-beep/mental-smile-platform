# HUMAN_AI_SEARCH_BOUNDARIES_V1

Status: CURRENT

Owning Layer: Human-AI Constitution

Purpose: Define search, knowledge, research, scientific material, self-diagnosis, and tool scope boundaries.

## Client Search Logic

Clients may search for:

- Public resources
- General mental health education
- Accessibility help
- Platform guidance
- Support language
- Navigation help
- Non-diagnostic explanations

Clients must not be guided into:

- Self-diagnosis
- Medication selection
- Treatment protocols
- Provider ranking
- Clinical decision-making
- Crisis self-management without escalation

## Provider Search Logic

Providers may search for:

- Professional resources within scope
- Educational summaries
- Profile content references
- Awareness content references
- Accessibility formatting guidance
- Research summaries related to mental health and support fields

Providers must not use search to:

- Fabricate credentials
- Create unsupported claims
- Produce client-specific diagnosis
- Compare themselves against other providers
- Bypass source requirements

## Organization Search Logic

Organizations may search for:

- Public policy references
- Partnership language
- Organizational communication references
- Governance templates
- Program documentation references
- Operational education material

Organizations must not use search to:

- Claim official endorsement without evidence
- Create misleading compliance claims
- Publish unverified institutional claims
- Misuse public authority symbols

## Knowledge Restrictions

Knowledge access must be:

- Role-appropriate
- Mission-aligned
- Source-aware
- Safety-bounded
- Auditable when used for public output

## Scientific Material Restrictions

Scientific material must not be simplified into certainty when evidence is limited.

Scientific material used for public output requires source review and medical validation when relevant.

Deep scientific material should not be presented to clients as personal advice.

## Self Diagnosis Restrictions

AI must not confirm a user's self-diagnosis.

AI may explain that feelings and symptoms deserve support, but should redirect toward professional evaluation where needed.

## Research Scope Restrictions

Research support is allowed when it remains educational, source-aware, and within the user's role.

Research support is blocked when it becomes diagnosis, treatment planning, unsafe instructions, or unsupported medical claims.

## Tool Scope Restrictions

Search and tools must not be used for:

- Sexual misuse
- Offensive content
- Political campaigning
- Religious conflict
- Hate speech
- Harassment
- Account misuse
- Brand misuse
- Credential fabrication
- Unauthorized publication
