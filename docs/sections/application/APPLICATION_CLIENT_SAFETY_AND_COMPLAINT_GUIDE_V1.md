## Purpose

This guide defines client-facing safety and complaint guidance for the Application Section.

It does not create complaint runtime.

It does not collect complaint text.

It does not investigate.

It does not judge legal or professional matters.

## Client Safety Guidance

Clients should:

- verify provider identity and professional documents before agreement;
- verify center identity, official address, and communication channels;
- agree on service details before paying, attending, or sharing sensitive information;
- keep important agreements in written messages;
- avoid private meetings outside professional or known service locations when possible;
- avoid sharing unnecessary personal data;
- use official complaint links for legal, professional, licensing, or regulatory matters;
- use platform WhatsApp only for platform-related issues.

## Platform Identity Boundary

Mental Smile is not:

- police;
- court;
- regulator;
- clinic;
- legal authority;
- licensing body;
- emergency service.

Mental Smile may guide users toward official paths, but it does not replace official authorities.

## Complaint Boundary Rule

The platform handles only platform-related issues:

- fake page inside the platform;
- abusive platform-managed communication;
- offensive content inside platform-owned spaces;
- technical or support issue;
- suggestion or feedback.

The platform only guides for:

- criminal complaints;
- harassment, assault, threats, or extortion;
- session misconduct;
- professional misconduct;
- center misconduct;
- licensing or regulatory complaints.

## Forbidden Complaint Handling

The Application Section must not:

- investigate criminal matters;
- make legal judgment;
- handle criminal reports;
- store personal complaint details inside Application Aggregation;
- collect complaint evidence as an Application aggregation payload;
- route complaints directly to Legal Governance without an approved governance flow.

## WhatsApp Boundary

Platform WhatsApp is for platform-related support, suggestions, and routing help only.

It must not become:

- legal intake;
- criminal complaint storage;
- professional investigation channel;
- medical advice channel.

## Application Aggregation Complaint Rule

Application Aggregation may count platform-related issue categories only if a future approved signal exists.

It must never store:

- complaint text;
- names;
- phone numbers;
- email addresses;
- evidence files;
- medical or legal details;
- WhatsApp message content.

## Official Guidance Rule

For legal/professional matters, the Application Section may show official complaint links or guidance text only.

No investigation.

No judgment.

No case management.
