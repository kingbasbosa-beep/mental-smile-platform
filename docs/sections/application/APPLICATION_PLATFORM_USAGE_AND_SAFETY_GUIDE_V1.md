## What Mental Smile Is

Mental Smile is:

- support;
- knowledge;
- resources;
- discovery;
- accessibility;
- community access.

## What Mental Smile Is Not

Mental Smile is not:

- medical provider;
- therapy provider;
- emergency service;
- legal authority;
- police;
- court;
- regulator.

## Before Contacting Any Provider

Before contacting or agreeing with any provider, specialist, center, or organization:

- verify identity;
- verify licenses or professional documents where applicable;
- verify center information and address;
- use documented communication;
- keep important agreements written;
- review costs before commitment;
- do not rely only on verbal promises.

## Safety Rules

Users should:

- protect personal information;
- not share passwords;
- not share financial information publicly;
- avoid sending sensitive documents through public channels;
- use official authorities for urgent legal, criminal, or safety matters.

## Platform Complaints

Platform-related complaints may be submitted through official Mental Smile channels.

Examples:

- abuse inside platform-owned spaces;
- harassment inside platform-managed communication;
- fake profiles inside the platform;
- misleading information inside platform-owned spaces;
- platform misuse;
- technical support issues;
- suggestions and feedback.

## Legal / Criminal Cases

Mental Smile does not investigate crimes.

For the following, users should contact official authorities, emergency services, professional regulators, or relevant government entities:

- threats;
- blackmail;
- fraud;
- sexual harassment;
- violence;
- child abuse;
- medical malpractice;
- criminal complaints;
- licensing or regulatory complaints.

## Content Rules

Mental Smile Application surfaces must not promote:

- medical advice as diagnosis or treatment;
- treatment claims;
- miracle cures;
- provider guarantees;
- misleading promises;
- unsafe content.

## Google Play Compliance

Application content and communication should follow:

- respectful communication;
- no hate speech;
- no harassment;
- no dangerous content;
- no illegal activity;
- no impersonation.

## Data Boundary

This guide does not create:

- complaint runtime;
- complaint collection;
- Firebase rules;
- legal workflow;
- monitoring workflow;
- administrative workflow.
