# LIBRARY CONSTITUTION V1

Operation ID: OP-LIBRARY-CONSTITUTION-AND-GOVERNANCE-BLOCK-V1

Status: ACTIVE_SECTION_CONSTITUTION

Section: Library

Runtime effect: none

## Purpose

The Library Section is a governed content and resource system.

It exists to provide:

- public knowledge;
- guided resources;
- learning material;
- official references;
- specialist-only references;
- external references;
- Mental Smile authored or reviewed content.

The Library Section is not:

- a diagnosis system;
- a treatment system;
- an open upload system;
- a clinical education replacement;
- a booking system;
- a payment system;
- a provider verification system;
- a legal or clinical decision system.

## Section Boundary

Library owns:

- content categories;

- official links;
- learning material;
- public knowledge;
- specialist-only references;
- research references;

- legal decisions;
- clinical decisions;

- user-generated medical content.

## Client-Facing Safety Rule

Every client-facing mental-health content item must follow this rule:

Arabic:

تشابه الأعراض أثناء التصفح لا يعني وجود تشخيص.

التقييم والتشخيص يجب أن يتم فقط من خلال مختص مؤهل.

English:

Reading similar symptoms does not mean you have a diagnosis.

Assessment and diagnosis must only be done by a qualified professional.

## Client Contribution Boundary

Clients cannot upload content directly into the Library.

Clients may only:

- suggest a topic;
- suggest a link;
- suggest a resource;
- request content.

Mental Smile may then review, source, create, reject, or archive the request.

## AI Content Boundary

AI-assisted Library content may exist only when governed by:

- original prompt;
- prompt version;
- generation purpose;
- reviewer;
- review status;
- ownership statement;
- safety notes;
- prompt card.

No AI-generated Library content may exist without a prompt card.

## Card Governance System

The Library Card System is active.

Every governed Library item must have the relevant card coverage:

- Content Card;
- Category Card;
- Source Card;
- Prompt Card when Mental Smile original or AI-assisted;
- Localization Card for visible labels;
- Signal Card for future summary-only signal families.
