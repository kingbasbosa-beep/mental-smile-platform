# LIBRARY CONTENT SAFETY DOCTRINE V1

Operation ID: OP-LIBRARY-CONSTITUTION-AND-GOVERNANCE-BLOCK-V1

Status: ACTIVE_LIBRARY_DOCTRINE

Runtime effect: none

## Client-Facing Mental Health Safety Rule

All client-facing mental-health content must include or follow:

Arabic:

تشابه الأعراض أثناء التصفح لا يعني وجود تشخيص.

التقييم والتشخيص يجب أن يتم فقط من خلال مختص مؤهل.

English:

Reading similar symptoms does not mean you have a diagnosis.

Assessment and diagnosis must only be done by a qualified professional.

## Forbidden Content Behavior

Library content must not:

- diagnose the reader;
- imply guaranteed treatment outcome;
- present unreviewed medical claims as fact;
- replace qualified professional assessment;
- encourage unsafe self-treatment;
- invite users to upload medical documents directly;
- make legal, clinical, or emergency decisions.

## Client Safety Requirements

Client-facing content should:

- use simple language;
- distinguish information from diagnosis;
- encourage qualified support when needed;
- identify external sources;
- avoid fear-based wording;
- avoid miracle cure claims.

## Specialist-Only Safety Boundary

Specialist-only content may be technical, but must be clearly marked and not exposed as general client guidance.

## Review Requirement

Safety review status is mandatory metadata for Library content.

Unreviewed content must remain Internal Review Only or Archive Only.
