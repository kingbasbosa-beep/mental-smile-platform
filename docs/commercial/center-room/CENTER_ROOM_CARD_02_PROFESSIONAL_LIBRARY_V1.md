## Current Mission

Provide a structured static professional reference library for therapeutic, rehabilitation, educational, and service centers.

## Purpose

The card opens a professional reference surface for center operators.

It helps centers reach external references for government bodies, accreditation, quality, safety, infection control, mental health, addiction treatment, operations, emergency readiness, training, research, digital health, humanitarian organizations, funding, conferences, and policies.
