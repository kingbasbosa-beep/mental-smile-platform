## New Administrative Philosophy

Administrative is not control.

Administrative is not approval authority.

Administrative is not centralized operations.

Administrative is not hidden command power.

Administrative is coordination, planning, compliance, visibility, ownership separation, and department operations.

## Owner

### Purpose

Sovereign decision making, long-term direction, and final ownership authority.

### Mission

Hold final ownership clarity for Mental Smile and preserve long-term direction without becoming an operational management layer.

### Responsibilities

- Define sovereign decisions.
- Preserve long-term direction.
- Confirm final ownership authority.
- Maintain ownership separation across departments.
- Resolve final direction when department boundaries conflict.

### Boundaries

- Owner authority is final direction, not day-to-day operations.
- Owner authority does not create hidden administrative command power.
- Owner authority does not replace departmental responsibility.

### Ownership

Owner owns sovereign decisions, long-term direction, and final ownership authority.

### Non-Responsibilities

- Operational management.
- Monitoring.
- Technical execution.
- Legal drafting.
- Accounting execution.
- Marketing operations.

### Cross-Department Relationships

- Receives strategic recommendations from Strategic Planning & Development.
- Receives compliance and risk review from Legal.
- Receives financial visibility from Accounting.
- Receives brand and visibility context from Marketing & Media.
- Receives platform reliability context from Technical Operations.

## Strategic Planning & Development

### Purpose

Growth planning, expansion planning, future initiatives, research, and strategic recommendations.

### Mission

Translate Mental Smile direction into structured future planning without taking legal, technical, or owner authority.

### Responsibilities

- Plan growth.
- Plan expansion.
- Research future initiatives.
- Prepare strategic recommendations.
- Coordinate strategic development context between departments.

### Boundaries

- Strategic Planning & Development recommends; it does not command.
- Strategic planning does not approve legal policy.
- Strategic planning does not execute technical systems.
- Strategic planning does not override owner authority.

### Ownership

Strategic Planning & Development owns planning, research, expansion thinking, and strategic recommendation preparation.

### Non-Responsibilities

- Legal authority.
- Technical authority.
- Owner authority.
- Accounting governance.
- Marketing execution.
- Runtime implementation.

### Cross-Department Relationships

- Sends recommendations to Owner.
- Requests compliance review from Legal.
- Requests financial visibility from Accounting.
- Coordinates market-facing planning context with Marketing & Media.
- Requests feasibility context from Technical Operations.
