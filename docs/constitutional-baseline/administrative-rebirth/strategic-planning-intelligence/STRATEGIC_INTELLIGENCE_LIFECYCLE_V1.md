# Strategic Intelligence Lifecycle V1

Operation ID: OP-STRATEGIC-PLANNING-INTELLIGENCE-ARCHITECTURE-V1  
Date: 2026-06-25

## Status

ARCHITECTURE_ONLY

## Scope

This document defines the future lifecycle of strategic intelligence.

No workflow engine is created.

No runtime is created.

No report generation is created.

## Intelligence Creation

Strategic intelligence begins when allowed inputs are received, researched, observed, or drafted.

Creation must preserve source context, date context, and whether the source is human, AI-assisted, archive-derived, or department-derived.

## Intelligence Review

Every intelligence item requires human review before publication.

Review determines whether the item is draft, usable, blocked, retired, or ready for future publication.

## Intelligence Classification

Classification may include:

- Risk
- Opportunity
- Growth
- Technology
- Cross Department
- Forecast
- Research
- Weekly Planning
- Historical Intelligence

Classification does not create authority.

## Intelligence Publication

Publication means preparing intelligence for an approved future destination.

Publication cannot occur without human review.

Publication does not approve platform changes.

## Intelligence Archival

Archival means preserving approved intelligence in a future approved archive destination.

No archive connection is created in this block.

## Intelligence Expiration

Intelligence may expire when its assumptions, source context, timing, or strategic relevance are no longer valid.

Expired intelligence may remain historically valuable but must not be used as current guidance.

## Intelligence Revalidation

Revalidation checks whether expired, old, uncertain, or disputed intelligence remains useful.

Revalidation must identify what changed and whether the intelligence should return to active planning use.

## Intelligence Retirement

Retirement removes intelligence from active planning use while preserving historical trace when appropriate.

Retired intelligence must identify the reason for retirement.

## Boundary

This lifecycle defines future governance only.

No lifecycle system is implemented by this document.
