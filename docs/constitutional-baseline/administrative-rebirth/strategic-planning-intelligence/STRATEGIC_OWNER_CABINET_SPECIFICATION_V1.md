## Purpose

The future Owner Strategic Cabinet is the owner-facing destination for reviewed strategic intelligence, recommendations, forecasts, risk reports, opportunity reports, and planning packages.

- Weekly Planning Packages
- Monthly Strategic Reviews

## Outputs

- Owner review material
- Owner direction notes

- Archive-ready owner-approved strategic material

## Storage Rules

- Owner Cabinet storage is future-only and not implemented.
- Records must distinguish reviewed intelligence from owner decision material.
- Strategic Planning & Intelligence may not write owner authority.
- Owner-facing packages must preserve source trace.

## Retention Rules

- Owner-facing reviewed packages are retained according to future Owner Department governance.
- Approved historical copies may be sent to future Approved Historical Storage.
- Drafts must not be treated as final owner material.

## Read Access

- Owner has primary read access.
- Strategic Planning & Intelligence may receive future limited read access for prepared packages if owner governance allows.
- Archive may receive approved historical copies only after future approval rules exist.

## Write Access

- Owner write access is governed by future Owner Department implementation.
- Strategic Planning & Intelligence may prepare reviewed packages but may not create owner authority records.
- AI may not write directly to Owner Cabinet.

## Relationship To Archive

Owner Strategic Cabinet may send approved historical packages to Archive Strategic Planning Vault or Approved Historical Storage in future architecture.

No Archive connection is created in this block.

## Relationship To Weekly Meeting

Weekly Planning Packages may become source material for future Owner Strategic Cabinet review.

No weekly meeting package runtime is created in this block.
