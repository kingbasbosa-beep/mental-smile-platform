# Strategic AI Governance V1

Operation ID: OP-STRATEGIC-PLANNING-INTELLIGENCE-ARCHITECTURE-V1  
Date: 2026-06-25

## Status

ARCHITECTURE_ONLY

## Scope

This document defines AI governance for Strategic Planning & Intelligence.

No AI engine is created.

No analytics engine is created.

No report generation system is created.

No tool is connected.

## Allowed AI Actions

AI may:

- Analyze
- Classify
- Summarize
- Compare
- Forecast
- Recommend
- Detect patterns
- Generate drafts

## Forbidden AI Actions

AI may not:

- Approve
- Authorize
- Execute
- Deploy
- Modify governance
- Modify archive
- Modify runtime
- Modify Firebase
- Modify Firestore
- Modify Storage
- Override humans
- Override Owner
- Override Legal
- Override Technical Operations

## Human Review Rules

- Every AI output must be reviewed by a human before publication.
- Every AI recommendation must remain non-authoritative.
- Every AI forecast must include uncertainty and source context before use.
- Every AI-generated draft must be labeled as draft until reviewed.
- Legal, technical, financial, and governance-sensitive outputs require domain review before use.

## AI Report Types

- AI Intelligence Report
- Hybrid Intelligence Report
- AI Forecast Draft
- AI Risk Draft
- AI Opportunity Draft
- AI Research Summary
- AI Comparison Summary
- AI Pattern Detection Note

## AI Escalation Rules

- Escalate to Legal when AI identifies legal, compliance, terms, privacy, or policy risk.
- Escalate to Technical Operations when AI identifies reliability, deployment, infrastructure, or technical risk.
- Escalate to Owner when AI identifies strategic direction risk, major opportunity, or ownership-level decision need.
- Escalate to Strategic Planning & Intelligence human review when AI confidence is low or source conflict exists.

## AI Confidence Rules

- AI outputs must not be treated as fact without source review.
- AI confidence must be classified as HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW, or UNKNOWN.
- LOW confidence outputs may be used only as research leads.
- UNKNOWN confidence outputs may not be published.
- Conflicting AI outputs require human reconciliation.

## AI Usage Boundaries

- AI supports intelligence work.
- AI does not create authority.
- AI does not create decisions.
- AI does not change records.
- AI does not connect systems.
- AI does not publish final reports without human review.

## AI Audit Requirements

- Record the AI task.
- Record the input source set.
- Record the generated output type.
- Record confidence classification.
- Record human reviewer status.
- Record publication status.
- Record archive status if later archived.

## Boundary

This governance document defines AI rules only.

No AI tool is installed, configured, or connected by this document.
