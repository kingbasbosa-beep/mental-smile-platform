# Strategic Lab Governance V1

Operation ID: OP-STRATEGIC-PLANNING-INTELLIGENCE-ARCHITECTURE-V1  
Date: 2026-06-25

## Status

ARCHITECTURE_ONLY

## Strategic Lab Purpose

The Strategic Lab is a future research and evaluation environment for Strategic Planning & Intelligence.

It exists to evaluate ideas, tools, forecasts, experiments, research directions, and strategic assumptions before they become recommendations.

No lab runtime is created in this block.

## Allowed Experiments

- Strategy hypothesis exploration
- Market and growth research
- Forecast comparison
- Risk modeling concepts
- Opportunity analysis concepts
- Technology evaluation concepts
- Documentation structure experiments
- AI-assisted analysis experiments
- Human research synthesis experiments

## Research Rules

- Research must preserve source context.
- Research must distinguish evidence from assumption.
- Research must identify uncertainty.
- Research must remain non-authoritative.
- Research must not modify governance, archive, runtime, Firebase, Firestore, or Storage.

## Technology Evaluation Rules

- Technology evaluation may assess purpose, benefits, risks, constraints, and future fit.
- Technology evaluation may not install, connect, deploy, or configure tools in this block.
- Technology evaluation must route technical feasibility questions to Technical Operations in future governance.

## Proof Of Concept Rules

- Proof of concept work is not created in this block.
- Future proof of concept work must be isolated from production.
- Future proof of concept work must not create platform authority.
- Future proof of concept work must not modify runtime without a separate approved implementation block.

## Documentation Rules

- Lab work must be documented before it becomes a recommendation.
- Drafts must be labeled as drafts.
- Reviewed material must identify reviewer status.
- Retired experiments must preserve reason for retirement.

## Permitted Tools

Future permitted tools:

- GPT
- Codex
- Android Studio
- Analytics Platforms
- Research Systems
- Knowledge Bases
- Forecasting Systems
- Documentation Systems

No tool is installed, configured, or connected in this block.

## Forbidden Activities

- Runtime Changes
- Production Changes
- Firebase Changes
- Firestore Changes
- Storage Changes
- Governance Changes
- Archive Changes
- Deployment
- Route creation
- UI creation
- Dashboard creation
- Analytics engine creation

## Boundary

This document defines Strategic Lab governance only.

No Strategic Lab implementation is created.
