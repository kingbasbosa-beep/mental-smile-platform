# Owner Report Registry V1

## Status

FOUNDATION REGISTRY ONLY

NO RUNTIME REGISTRY CREATED

## Purpose

Define the Owner Department report categories for future owner-level records.

## Registry

| Report Category | Purpose | Accepted Inputs | Allowed Outputs | Owner Bucket | Runtime Created |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner Notes | Preserve owner-originated notes and observations. | Owner Notes | Owner Review Material / Owner Direction preparation note | OWNER | NO |
| Owner Directions | Preserve final direction from Owner. | Owner Review Material / Owner Notes | Owner Direction Records | OWNER | NO |
| Owner Reviews | Preserve owner-level review of submitted material. | Strategic Reports / Weekly Planning Packages / Quarterly Reviews / Risk Reports / Opportunity Reports | Owner Review Material | OWNER | NO |
| Owner Decisions | Preserve final owner decision references. | Owner Reviews / Owner Notes | Owner Decision References | OWNER | NO |
| Strategic Reviews | Preserve owner review of strategic intelligence. | Strategic Reports / Opportunity Reports / Risk Reports | Approved Strategic References / Owner Review Material | OWNER | NO |
| Weekly Meeting Reviews | Preserve owner-level review of weekly planning packages. | Weekly Planning Packages | Owner Review Material / Owner Direction Records | OWNER | NO |

## Registry Rules

- Registry categories do not create runtime records.
- Registry categories do not create Firebase collections.
- Registry categories do not create routes.
- Registry categories do not create UI.
- Registry categories do not restore Administrative V1.

## Ownership

All report categories in this registry are constitutionally owned by OWNER when created as owner-level records.
