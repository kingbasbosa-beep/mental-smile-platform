## Constitutional Identity

The Owner Department is the sovereign destination for reviewed intelligence, strategic reports, planning packages, ownership notes, and long-term direction records.

The Owner Department is not the old Administrative Section.

The Owner Department does not restore Administrative V1.

The Owner Department does not create a hidden administrative authority layer.

## Purpose

Sovereign decision making, long-term direction, final ownership authority, and preservation of reviewed strategic direction records.

## Mission

Receive reviewed strategic intelligence and planning material, preserve owner-level direction records, and define final ownership references without becoming operational management, monitoring, technical execution, legal execution, accounting execution, marketing execution, or centralized administrative control.

## Responsibilities

- Own sovereign long-term direction.
- Own final ownership authority.
- Receive reviewed strategic intelligence.
- Receive weekly planning packages when eligible for owner review.
- Receive quarterly reviews when eligible for owner review.
- Receive risk reports after review by the originating department.
- Receive opportunity reports after review by the originating department.
- Preserve owner notes.
- Preserve owner direction records.
- Preserve approved strategic references.

## Boundaries

- Owner does not operate daily platform workflows.
- Owner does not perform monitoring.
- Owner does not perform technical execution.
- Owner does not perform legal execution.
- Owner does not perform accounting execution.
- Owner does not perform marketing execution.
- Owner does not replace departmental ownership.
- Owner does not recreate Administrative dashboards, queues, approval chains, or command rooms.
- Owner does not create runtime, routes, UI, Firebase, Firestore, or Storage structures in this foundation.

## Ownership

Owner owns sovereign decisions, long-term direction, final ownership authority, owner notes, owner direction records, owner decisions, owner reviews, and approved strategic references.

## Non-Responsibilities

- Operational management.
- Runtime execution.
- Technical maintenance.
- Legal policy drafting.
- Accounting records maintenance.
- Marketing production.
- Support queue handling.
- Registry governance execution.
- Administrative V1 restoration.

## Accepted Inputs

- Strategic Reports.
- Weekly Planning Packages.
- Quarterly Reviews.
- Risk Reports.
- Opportunity Reports.
- Owner Notes.

## Allowed Outputs

- Owner Review Material.
- Owner Direction Records.
- Approved Strategic References.

## Cross-Department Relationships

- Receives reviewed strategic intelligence from Strategic Planning and Intelligence.
- Receives risk and compliance context from Legal when required.
- Receives financial visibility and planning context from Accounting when required.
- Receives brand and public visibility context from Marketing and Media when required.
- Receives feasibility and reliability context from Technical Operations when required.
- Sends final direction references back to departments only as owner direction records.

## Archive Relationship

Owner may preserve approved strategic references that cite Archive evidence.

Owner does not modify Archive evidence.

Owner does not overwrite historical records.
