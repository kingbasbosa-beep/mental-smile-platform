# Owner Strategic Cabinet V1

## Status

SPECIFICATION FOUNDATION ONLY

NO IMPLEMENTATION

NO RUNTIME

NO UI

NO FIREBASE

## Purpose

Final destination for reviewed strategic intelligence.

## Inputs

- Strategic Reports.
- Weekly Planning Packages.
- Quarterly Reviews.
- Risk Reports.
- Opportunity Reports.
- Owner Notes.

## Outputs

- Owner Review Material.
- Owner Direction Records.
- Approved Strategic References.

## Cabinet Role

The Owner Strategic Cabinet is the constitutional destination where reviewed strategic intelligence becomes owner-level review material, owner direction records, or approved strategic references.

## Admission Rules

- Material must be reviewed before owner cabinet admission.
- Material must identify its originating department or source.
- Material must preserve ownership metadata.
- Material must preserve governance metadata when present.
- Material must not be admitted as a runtime queue.
- Material must not create Administrative V1 approval flow.

## Review Requirements

- Strategic Reports require Strategic Planning and Intelligence origin or review.
- Weekly Planning Packages require weekly planning package status.
- Quarterly Reviews require quarterly review classification.
- Risk Reports require risk classification.
- Opportunity Reports require opportunity classification.
- Owner Notes require Owner origin.

## Ownership Rules

- Owner owns cabinet records after admission.
- Originating departments retain ownership of their source material unless converted into owner direction records.
- Archive evidence remains owned by Archive when referenced.

## Relationship To Archive

The cabinet may reference Archive evidence.

The cabinet does not mutate Archive evidence.

The cabinet does not replace Archive custody.

## Relationship To Weekly Planning

Weekly Planning Packages may enter the cabinet only when they are reviewed and elevated for owner-level consideration.

## Dependency Status

Strategic Planning and Intelligence foundation exists.

Owner Department operational foundation exists through this package.

No runtime dependency is created by this document.
