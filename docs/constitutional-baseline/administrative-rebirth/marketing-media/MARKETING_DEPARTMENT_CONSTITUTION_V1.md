# Marketing And Media Department Constitution V1

## Status

CONSTITUTION CREATION

NO RUNTIME

NO UI

NO FIREBASE

## Operation

OP-MARKETING-DEPARTMENT-CONSTITUTION-EXTRACTION-V1

## Source Basis

This department is created from extracted reality only:

- Commercial V2 forensic audits.
- Archive commercial production chain audit.
- Marketing Machine extraction audit.
- Administrative Rebirth foundation.

## Purpose

Receive Commercial V2 profile and product assets, prepare them for public communication, generate campaign-ready derivatives, coordinate publication material, track performance context, report outcomes, and preserve source plus generated evidence through Archive.

## Mission

Bridge Commercial V2 asset production, Archive custody, and future public visibility without becoming Commercial runtime, Owner authority, Legal authority, Technical execution, payment execution, booking execution, or Administrative authority.

## Responsibilities

- Receive Commercial V2 published profile references and related assets.
- Prepare profile and asset packages for public communication.
- Generate or coordinate derivative marketing assets when tools exist.
- Coordinate campaign-ready packages when tools exist.
- Maintain publication material context.
- Track marketing performance context when tracking exists.
- Produce marketing reports when reporting exists.
- Preserve marketing source and generated assets through Archive destinations.

## Boundaries

- Marketing and Media does not create Commercial profiles.
- Marketing and Media does not verify providers or centers.
- Marketing and Media does not manage bookings.
- Marketing and Media does not manage payments.
- Marketing and Media does not override Owner.
- Marketing and Media does not execute Legal authority.
- Marketing and Media does not replace Archive custody.
- Marketing and Media does not create Administrative authority.
- Marketing and Media does not create runtime, routes, UI, Firebase, Firestore, or Storage in this foundation.

## Ownership

Marketing and Media owns marketing preparation context, campaign material context, publication material context, media output context, channel context, and marketing performance report context when those records are created.

Archive owns long-term custody of archived source and generated assets.

Commercial owns Commercial V2 profile production.

Owner owns final owner-level direction when required.

Legal owns legal policy and compliance review.

## Non-Responsibilities

- Commercial V2 profile creation.
- Commercial V2 account registration.
- Commercial verification.
- Clinical, booking, payment, or case management functions.
- Legal policy.
- Owner decisions.
- Technical deployment.
- Archive custody replacement.

## Cross-Department Relationships

- Receives source assets from Commercial V2.
- Stores and retrieves source/generated evidence through Archive.
- Sends strategic marketing summaries to Strategic Planning and Intelligence when reporting exists.
- Sends owner review material to Owner when elevated.
- Receives legal/compliance boundaries from Legal when content risk exists.
- Receives technical feasibility/support from Technical Operations when publication tools exist.
