# Marketing Owner Handoff V1

## Operation

OP-MARKETING-OPERATIONAL-PIPELINE-FOUNDATION-V1

## Status

FOUNDATION ONLY

NO IMPLEMENTATION

## Purpose

Define how Marketing and Media material may reach Owner when elevated through Strategic Planning and Intelligence or direct owner review context.

## Eligible Owner Handoff Material

- Strategic Marketing Summary Package.
- Campaign Performance Summary.
- Commercial Product Visibility Summary.
- High-impact publication risk or opportunity note.
- Owner-requested marketing review material.

## Destination

Owner Strategic Cabinet.

## Required Metadata

- Source marketing report.
- Strategic handoff reference.
- Archive reference.
- Commercial source profile references.
- Review status.
- Owner question or decision context when present.

## Owner Handoff Rules

- Owner handoff creates owner review material only.
- Owner handoff does not create Owner Direction Records unless Owner acts separately.
- Owner handoff must preserve Archive custody.
- Owner handoff must not execute Legal authority.
- Owner handoff must not create Administrative approval chains.
