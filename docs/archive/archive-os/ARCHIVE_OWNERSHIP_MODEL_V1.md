# Archive Ownership Model V1

Operation ID: OP-ARCHIVE-OS-FOUNDATION-BLOCKS-A-H-V1

Date: 2026-06-25

## Who Owns Archive

Archive is owned by the Archive section.

Departments do not own Archive.

Owner does not replace Archive.

Administrative does not own Archive.

Departments own vault spaces inside Archive.

## Who Reads Archive

Read consumers include:

- Owner;
- Strategic Planning;
- Technical;
- Marketing;
- Legal;
- Accounting;
- Application;
- Library;
- Commercial;
- future governed sections.

## Who Depends On Archive

Archive is depended on by:

- recovery workflows;
- strategic planning;
- constitutional governance;
- prompt recovery;
- failure prevention;
- historical reporting;
- provider portfolio reuse;
- marketing content reuse;
- future migration operations.

## Who Prevents Deletion

Deletion is prohibited by default.

Deletion can only be considered through a future explicitly approved constitutional exception.

Archive records may be frozen, superseded, retired, or moved into protected history.

Silent deletion is forbidden.

## Who Approves Restoration

Restoration requires:

- source record;
- related operation record;
- ownership confirmation;
- recovery rule reference;
- Owner approval if sovereign assets or platform-critical records are involved.

## Ownership Boundary

Archive owns memory.

Owner owns sovereign decision authority.

Strategic Planning owns analysis.

Technical owns implementation and recovery execution.

Archive preserves all of the above without becoming them.
