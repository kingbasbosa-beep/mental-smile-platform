## Executive Summary

Commercial V2 can currently produce published profiles, identity images, logos, official profile PDFs, and partial CV assets.

Archive has relevant receiving destinations, especially Provider Portfolio Vault, Marketing Content Library, Marketing Asset Vault, Strategic Planning Vault, and Owner Vault.

The chain is not operational because Commercial-specific archive contracts, registries, classifications, and handoff packages are missing.

## What Can Flow Now

- Published profile references can be manually mapped to Provider Portfolio Vault.
- Identity images and logos can be manually mapped to Provider Portfolio Vault and Marketing Asset Vault.
- Generated PDFs and CV assets can be manually archived if exported and registered.
- Commercial forensic reports can be stored as Archive/Strategic evidence.

## What Cannot Flow Yet

- Automated profile archive packages.
- Automated marketing campaign packages.
- Business cards.
- Promo posts.
- Success stories.
- Strategic commercial summaries.
- Commercial aggregation outputs.

## Highest Priority Chain Gap

Commercial V2 profile output does not yet have a formal archive receiving contract.
