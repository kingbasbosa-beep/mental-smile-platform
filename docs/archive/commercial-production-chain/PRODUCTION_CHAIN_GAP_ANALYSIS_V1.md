# Production Chain Gap Analysis V1

## Operation

OP-ARCHIVE-COMMERCIAL-PRODUCTION-CHAIN-AUDIT-V1

## Status

FORENSIC AUDIT ONLY

## Missing Links

| Missing Link | Impact |
|---|---|
| Commercial V2 profile-to-Provider Portfolio Vault contract | Published profiles cannot become governed portfolio archive packages automatically. |
| Commercial V2 output asset registry | Identity images, logos, PDFs, CVs, future cards/posts lack Commercial-specific archive registration. |
| Marketing Department | Future publisher is not created yet. |
| Marketing handoff package | Commercial outputs cannot become campaign-ready packages. |
| Commercial aggregation summary | Commercial outputs cannot produce Strategic Planning Vault intelligence yet. |
| Commercial V2 discovery | Published profiles have no discovery/list/search distribution path. |

## Broken Links

| Broken Link | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Commercial V2 signals to aggregation | No Commercial V2 signal emitter found; monitoring code is disconnected. |
| Commercial V2 generated assets to Archive | PDF/CV outputs are generated on demand but not automatically stored or registered. |
| Commercial V2 to Marketing | Handoff is documented but Marketing Department and publication pipeline do not exist. |

## Unowned Links

| Link | Ownership Gap |
|---|---|
| Business card generation to archive | No generator owner and no archive intake class. |
| Promo post generation to Marketing Content Library | No generator owner and no publication owner. |
| Campaign package assembly | Marketing not created; Commercial output handoff not governed. |
| Success story intake | No consent/legal/content owner defined in active Marketing Department. |

## Unclassified Assets

- Business Cards.
- Promo Posts.
- Campaign Packages.
- Success Stories.
- Education Assets as campaign material.
- Training Assets as campaign material.
- Future Assets.

## Unarchived Assets

- Generated PDF assets unless manually saved.
- Generated CV preview/PDF assets unless manually saved.
- Identity images/logos as archive assets unless copied/registered into vaults.
- Published profile snapshots unless exported or registered.

## Unknown Destinations

- Future assets with no defined asset class.
- Success stories without Marketing/Legal classification.
- Campaign packages before Marketing Department creation.

## Gap Verdict

The production chain is ready for mapping, not execution.

The first durable connection should be Commercial V2 published profile to Provider Portfolio Vault, followed by Commercial V2 generated assets to Marketing Asset Vault and Marketing Content Library.
