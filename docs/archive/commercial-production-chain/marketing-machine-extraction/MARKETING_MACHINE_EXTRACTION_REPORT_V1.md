## What Is Marketing?

Marketing is the future machine that receives Commercial V2 profile/product assets, prepares them for public communication, generates campaign-ready derivatives, publishes approved material through defined channels, tracks performance, reports outcomes, and archives source plus generated evidence.

This audit does not create Marketing Department.

## Why Does It Exist?

Marketing exists because Commercial V2 can produce profile-centered assets, but those assets do not become campaign-ready, publication-ready, measurable, or archive-governed by themselves.

## What Problem Does It Solve?

It solves the gap between:

- Commercial V2 asset production.
- Archive custody and classification.
- Future public distribution.
- Campaign measurement.
- Strategic reporting.

## Exact Boundaries

Marketing Machine does:

- Receive Commercial outputs.
- Prepare assets.
- Generate derivative marketing assets.
- Publish approved material.
- Track performance.
- Report campaign/product outcomes.
- Archive source and generated assets.

Marketing Machine does not:

- Create Commercial profiles.
- Verify providers or centers.
- Manage bookings.
- Manage payments.
- Override Owner.
- Execute Legal authority.
- Replace Archive custody.
- Create Administrative authority.
