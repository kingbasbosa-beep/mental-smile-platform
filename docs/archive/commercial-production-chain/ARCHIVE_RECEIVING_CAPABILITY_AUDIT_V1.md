## Status

## Archive Destination Capability Matrix

| Archive Destination | Can Receive | Cannot Receive | Missing Contracts | Missing Registries | Missing Storage Rules | Missing Classifications |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Provider Portfolio Vault | Published profile references, identity images, logos, PDF/CV assets when exported and registered | Automated live profile ingestion, unreviewed campaign packages | Commercial V2 profile-to-portfolio writer contract | Commercial V2 portfolio asset registry entries | Commercial V2 profile snapshot storage rules | Published profile, CV asset, identity media, version snapshot classes |
| Marketing Content Library | Profile copy, campaign text, promo post content when created and reviewed | Raw profile drafts, unreviewed success stories, generated posts that do not exist | Commercial-to-Marketing content handoff contract | Commercial campaign/content registry entries | Commercial marketing content intake rules | Promo post, campaign package, success story, education/training content classes |
| Marketing Asset Vault | Logos, identity images, future business cards, future post visuals | Raw Storage files without asset cards, missing business cards/posts | Commercial asset handoff contract | Commercial asset registry entries | Asset card and versioning rules for Commercial outputs | Identity image, logo, business card, post visual, campaign visual classes |
| Strategic Planning Vault | Commercial product audits, strategic summaries, future Commercial aggregation outputs | Raw unreviewed Commercial signals or profile drafts | Commercial strategic summary writer contract | Commercial strategic report registry entries | Strategic summary storage rules for Commercial source packages | Commercial summary, demand signal, discovery trend, lead-generation classes |
| Owner Vault | Owner-approved Commercial references, owner review material, final direction records | Routine profile drafts, raw campaign assets, unreviewed signals | Owner Commercial review handoff contract | Owner Commercial review registry entries | Owner review material storage rules | Owner commercial decision, owner commercial review, approved commercial reference classes |

## Capability Findings

- Provider Portfolio Vault is the closest receiving destination for current Commercial V2 outputs.
- Marketing Content Library and Marketing Asset Vault can conceptually receive materials but require Commercial-specific handoff contracts and registries.
- Strategic Planning Vault can receive Commercial intelligence only after Commercial aggregation or summaries exist.
- Owner Vault can receive reviewed Commercial decision material, not raw production output.
