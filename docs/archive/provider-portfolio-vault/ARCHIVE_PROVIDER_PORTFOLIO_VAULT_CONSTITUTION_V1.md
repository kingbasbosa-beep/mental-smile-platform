# Archive Provider Portfolio Vault Constitution V1

Operation ID: OP-ARCHIVE-PROVIDER-PORTFOLIO-VAULT-FOUNDATION-V1  
Date: 2026-06-25

## Mission

Provider Portfolio Vault preserves provider-generated business assets as sovereign platform assets.

These assets support publication, recovery, reuse, historical preservation, and future template ecosystems.

## Scope

Provider Portfolio Vault may preserve:

- Business cards
- Generated CVs
- Portfolio PDFs
- Promotional posts
- Publication packages
- Future templates
- Future provider business assets

## Ownership

Provider Portfolio Assets are sovereign business assets.

Provider Portfolio Assets are not client data.

Archive remains the source of truth.

Publication platforms are distribution channels only.

## Consumers

- Commercial
- Marketing
- Strategic Planning
- Owner Vault
- Technical Recovery Vault
- Future template ecosystems

## Boundaries

Provider Portfolio Vault does not:

- Execute publication
- Create runtime
- Create routes
- Modify Firebase
- Create UI
- Execute marketing
- Create payment logic
- Store client data

## Relationship With Commercial

Commercial may generate provider business assets.

Provider Portfolio Vault preserves the generated asset record and version history.

## Relationship With Marketing

Marketing may review and publish approved assets.

Marketing platforms are distribution channels, not source of truth.

## Relationship With Strategic Planning

Strategic Planning may use portfolio history for growth and template ecosystem planning.

## Relationship With Owner Vault

Owner Vault may reference sovereign provider portfolio assets as platform business assets and recovery material.
