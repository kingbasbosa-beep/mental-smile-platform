# Archive Marketing Content Library Constitution V1

Operation ID: OP-ARCHIVE-MARKETING-CONTENT-LIBRARY-FOUNDATION-V1  
Date: 2026-06-25

## Mission

Marketing Content Library preserves all Mental Smile marketing and awareness content as sovereign marketing assets.

Content must remain recoverable, searchable, reusable, and publishable.

Archive remains the source of truth.

Publication channels are distribution channels only.

## Scope

Marketing Content Library may preserve:

- Awareness posts
- Recovery support posts
- Addiction recovery content
- Family support content
- Children support content
- Accessibility content
- Provider awareness content
- Center awareness content
- Educational content
- Community content
- Platform awareness content
- Campaign records
- Post cards
- Content versions
- Prompt and asset references

## Ownership

Marketing content is a sovereign marketing asset.

Archive owns the source-of-truth record.

Publication platforms only distribute content.

## Consumers

- Marketing Vault
- Strategic Planning
- Owner Vault
- Provider Portfolio Vault
- Future publication workflows
- Future template ecosystems

## Boundaries

Marketing Content Library does not:

- Publish content
- Create social media posts
- Create runtime
- Create routes
- Modify Firebase
- Create UI
- Store credentials

## Relationship With Marketing Vault

Marketing Vault preserves campaigns, media, reports, and public communication memory.

Marketing Content Library preserves content records, versions, post cards, and publication preparation structures.

## Relationship With Strategic Planning

Strategic Planning may study content categories, history, and publication readiness through archived records.

## Relationship With Owner Vault

Owner Vault may reference sovereign marketing content as platform communication memory.

## Relationship With Publication Channels

Publication channels are distribution channels only.

Archive remains the source of truth.

## Relationship With Marketing Asset Vault

Marketing Asset Vault preserves source-of-truth visual and media assets, asset cards, asset versions, and deployment-copy rules.

Marketing Content Library may reference asset cards and asset registry entries.

Runtime asset copies are not source-of-truth records.
