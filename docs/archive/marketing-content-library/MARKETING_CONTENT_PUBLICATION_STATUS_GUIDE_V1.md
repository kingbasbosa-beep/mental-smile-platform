# Marketing Content Publication Status Guide V1

Operation ID: OP-ARCHIVE-MARKETING-CONTENT-LIBRARY-FOUNDATION-V1  
Date: 2026-06-25

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Content is being prepared |
| Approved | Content approved for future use |
| Queued | Content prepared for publication scheduling |
| Published | Content has been distributed through a channel |
| Archived | Content preserved as historical content |
| Retired | Content no longer used as current publication material |

## Boundary

Statuses are archive records.

They do not execute publishing.
