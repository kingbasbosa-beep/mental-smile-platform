# Marketing Content Ownership Doctrine V1

Operation ID: OP-ARCHIVE-MARKETING-CONTENT-LIBRARY-FOUNDATION-V1  
Date: 2026-06-25

## Ownership Types

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Platform-owned content | Created for Mental Smile and owned by the platform |
| Externally sourced content | Content sourced from outside references |
| Licensed content | Content used under a license |
| AI-generated content | Content generated with AI assistance |
| Prompt-linked content | Content linked to a prompt record |
| Asset-linked content | Content linked to an image, video, or design asset |

## Rule

Ownership must be recorded before content becomes a reusable archive asset.

No credentials or private account data belongs in content ownership records.
