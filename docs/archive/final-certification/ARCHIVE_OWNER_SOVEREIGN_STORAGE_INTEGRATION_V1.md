Operation ID: OP-ARCHIVE-FINAL-CONSTITUTIONAL-CERTIFICATION-V1

## Purpose

Document the future reference-only relationship between Archive and Owner Sovereign Storage.

No credentials are stored.

No secrets are stored.

## Owner Sovereign Storage

Owner Sovereign Storage is the external sovereign storage reference layer for Owner-controlled backups, capsules, and critical governance preservation.

## Owner Sovereign Assets Capsule

The Owner Sovereign Assets Capsule may reference:

- Critical brand assets
- Legal identity assets
- Sovereign governance exports
- Recovery references
- External storage references

## Archive Sovereign Replica Capsule

Archive Sovereign Replica Capsule may preserve references to:

- Archive export packages
- Strategic Memory snapshots
- Technical Recovery snapshots
- Departmental vault indexes
- Operations ledger exports

## Export Rules

- Export packages must identify source vault.
- Export packages must identify date.
- Export packages must identify included indexes.
- Export packages must not include credentials or secrets.

## Import Rules

- Imports must identify source.
- Imports must identify date.
- Imports must identify target vault.
- Imported references must not overwrite active source files without Owner approval.

## Recovery Rules

- Recovery from sovereign storage requires Owner authority.
- Recovery must preserve operation record.
- Recovery must not bypass Technical Recovery Vault.
- Recovery must not bypass Failure Knowledge if a failure occurred.

## Owner Authority Rules

Owner controls sovereign storage references.

Archive records references and governance rules only.

Archive does not store passwords, tokens, API keys, or private credentials.

Owner Sovereign Integration: YES
