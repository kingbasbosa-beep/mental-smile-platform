# Archive Strategic Retention Rules V1

## Status

RETENTION RULES

NO DELETION

NO FIREBASE

## Purpose

Define retention expectations for strategic intelligence stored in Archive.

## Retention Principles

- Strategic intelligence is long-term institutional memory.
- Reviewed strategic records are retained as historical evidence.
- Owner-approved strategic references are retained as governing strategic history.
- Retention changes require a future explicit Archive retention operation.
- Retention rules do not authorize deletion.

## Retention Matrix

| Item Class | Retention | Deletion Authorized By This Document | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic Reports | Long-term | NO | Preserve reviewed strategic analysis. |
| Weekly Planning Packages | Long-term | NO | Preserve planning continuity and context. |
| Quarterly Reviews | Long-term | NO | Preserve periodic strategic history. |
| Risk Reports | Long-term | NO | Preserve risk context. |
| Opportunity Reports | Long-term | NO | Preserve opportunity context. |
| Owner-approved Strategic References | Long-term | NO | Preserve owner-approved references. |
| Strategic Direction Records | Long-term | NO | Preserve direction history. |
| Draft Strategic Material | Not eligible unless reviewed | NO | Must not be treated as permanent strategic evidence until reviewed. |

## Retention Boundary

This document does not delete, move, summarize, reduce, or alter existing records.
