# Archive Strategic History Registry V1

## Status

HISTORICAL REGISTRY

NO RUNTIME

## Purpose

Define how strategic intelligence becomes historical archive evidence.

## Historical Classification Registry

| History Class | Description | Eligible Items | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| STRATEGIC_ORIGIN_RECORD | First known source or origin of a strategic direction. | Foundational reports, planning origins, first owner references | Long-term |
| STRATEGIC_EVOLUTION_RECORD | Evidence showing how a strategic idea changed. | Revised reports, quarterly reviews, changed recommendations | Long-term |
| STRATEGIC_DECISION_CONTEXT | Context used for owner or departmental decision making. | Risk reports, opportunity reports, planning packages | Long-term |
| STRATEGIC_OUTCOME_REFERENCE | Historical record of known strategic outcome context. | Post-review notes, owner review material, approved references | Long-term |
| STRATEGIC_ARCHIVE_INDEX_ENTRY | Index-only reference to a strategic record stored elsewhere in Archive. | Cross-vault references | Long-term |

## History Rules

- Historical records preserve context.
- Historical records do not execute decisions.
- Historical records do not create department authority.
- Historical records do not override Owner direction records.
- Historical records may reference Archive evidence but must not duplicate credentials or private runtime secrets.

## Relationship To Strategic Memory

Strategic Planning Vault stores strategic intelligence packages.

Strategic Memory Vault preserves decision and evolution memory.

The two may reference each other but remain separate archive areas.
