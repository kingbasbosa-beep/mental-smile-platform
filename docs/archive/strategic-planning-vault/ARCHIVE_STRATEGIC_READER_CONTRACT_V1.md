# Archive Strategic Reader Contract V1

## Status

READER CONTRACT

NO RUNTIME ACCESS SYSTEM CREATED

## Purpose

Define read rules for the Archive Strategic Planning Vault.

## Archive Read Rules

- Readers may use vault material for historical understanding.
- Readers may use vault material for strategic review.
- Readers may use vault material for owner review preparation.
- Readers may use vault material for departmental context.
- Readers must not treat archive records as live runtime state.
- Readers must not treat archive records as operational commands.
- Readers must preserve source attribution.
- Readers must preserve ownership metadata.

## Eligible Reader Contexts

- Archive.
- Owner.
- Strategic Planning and Intelligence.
- Legal, for risk and compliance context.
- Accounting, for financial planning context.
- Marketing and Media, for campaign history context.
- Technical Operations, for feasibility and recovery context.

## Reader Boundaries

- Read access does not grant write access.
- Read access does not grant deletion authority.
- Read access does not authorize Firebase changes.
- Read access does not authorize route creation.
- Read access does not authorize dashboard creation.
