# Commercial V2 Identity Media Vs Marketing Media Doctrine

Date: 2026-06-21

## Doctrine

Mental Smile stores identity.

External platforms store marketing media.

Mental Smile stores references.

## Identity Media

Identity Media belongs to the entity itself and may remain part of the Commercial V2 profile source of truth.

Allowed identity assets:

- Specialist personal profile photo
- Center logo
- Organization logo
- Educational entity logo

Purpose:

- Identity
- Trust
- Public profile presentation
- Profile generation
- PDF generation
- Future business card generation

## Marketing Media

Marketing Media does not become a permanent gallery inside Mental Smile.

Examples:

- Room photos
- Facility photos
- Event photos
- Activity photos
- Promotional images
- Advertisements
- Brochures
- Videos
- Tours
- Awareness campaigns

Marketing assets may be used temporarily during a creation workflow, but after publication Mental Smile keeps only the external publication link.

## Workflow

Upload temporary marketing asset.

AI-assisted post creation if approved later.

Owner review.

Publish externally.

Store external URL only.

Remove temporary uploaded asset.

## Permanent Storage Policy

Allowed:

- Profile photo
- Logo

Not allowed as permanent Mental Smile gallery:

- Room images
- Facility albums
- Video files
- Promotional image libraries
- Internal media archives

## Public Profile Rule

Public profiles may show:

- Specialist profile photo
- Entity logo
- External media links

Public profiles must not become:

- Gallery system
- Media library
- Video hosting surface
- Internal social feed

## Final Verdict

COMMERCIAL_V2_IDENTITY_MEDIA_DOCTRINE_APPROVED_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION
