# Archive Marketing And Media Vault Constitution V1

Operation ID: OP-ARCHIVE-DEPARTMENTAL-VAULTS-AND-SHARED-RESOURCES-FOUNDATION-V1  
Date: 2026-06-24

## Purpose

Marketing and Media Vault preserves campaign and public communication memory.

## Contains

- Campaigns
- Posts
- Images
- Videos
- Reels
- Ads
- Designs
- Marketing Reports

## Supported Surfaces

- Facebook
- Instagram
- Telegram
- LinkedIn
- TikTok
- YouTube
- WhatsApp

## Boundary

Marketing and Media Vault stores references and governed media records.

It does not create social posting runtime.

## Marketing Content Library Relationship

Marketing Content Library preserves content records, post cards, versions, classification, ownership, campaign registries, and migration inventory.

Path:

`docs/archive/marketing-content-library/ARCHIVE_MARKETING_CONTENT_LIBRARY_CONSTITUTION_V1.md`

Archive remains source of truth.

Publication channels are distribution channels only.

## Marketing Asset Vault Relationship

Marketing Asset Vault preserves source-of-truth visual and media assets, asset cards, versions, and deployment-copy rules.

Marketing and Media may view archived assets and create new candidate assets.

Marketing and Media may not silently overwrite archived source assets.

Path:

`docs/archive/marketing-content-library/asset-vault/ARCHIVE_MARKETING_ASSET_VAULT_CONSTITUTION_V1.md`
