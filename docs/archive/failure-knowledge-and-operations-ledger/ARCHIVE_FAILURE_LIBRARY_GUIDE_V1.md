# Archive Failure Library Guide V1

Operation ID: OP-ARCHIVE-FAILURE-KNOWLEDGE-AND-OPERATIONS-LEDGER-V1  
Date: 2026-06-24

## Rule

Every significant failure must be registered.

Every significant fix must be registered.

Every significant lesson must be registered.

## Failure Record Minimum

Each failure record should include:

- What happened
- When it happened
- Why it happened
- Impact
- Fix applied
- Prevention
- Related reports
- Related operations
- Related guides
- Related cards

## Boundary

Failure records preserve knowledge.

They do not execute fixes.
